About 01

Tourism expertise,
event discipline.

With twenty years of event experience in the tourism industry, we set out on a new journey as PROMEX in 2018.

In the events we deliver, our most important goal is to be an effective solution partner that meets our clients' expectations with innovative and creative ideas. We think of our work as a symphony orchestra, and we position PROMEX as its conductor. Working in harmony with our stakeholders, with our professional approach and warm, friendly service, we make our stakeholders' satisfaction our greatest value. As the PROMEX brand, we add value to our clients' values, and we aim not to do better every day, but to do our best every day.
