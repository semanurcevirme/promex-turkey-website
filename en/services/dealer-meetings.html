Scope

What we offer

Dealer meetings strengthen the bond between your brand and your sales network. We clarify the goals, audience and key messages with you, then run the whole operation, from venue to transport, under one plan.

  • Venue selection and booking
  • Attendee registration and welcome
  • Stage, sound, lighting and video systems
  • Presentation flow and stage coordination
  • Accommodation and transfer management
  • Dinner and social programme
Process
  1. Brief & goalsWe clarify your expectations, audience and budget together.
  2. Planning & sourcingWe manage venues, equipment and suppliers on your behalf.
  3. DeliveryOur team coordinates the flow on site on the event day.
  4. ReviewWe review the results together and carry them into your next event.
